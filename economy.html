    Assumptions

    Nothing here is observed. Each choice adds a dashed line to the chart and a row to the readout, labelled as what it is.

    Hypothetical NPC liquidation

    Gold plus that share of NPC potential: what would reach the world if players sold that share of their loot to NPCs. No liquidation rate is known, so none is assumed by default.

    Constant creature set

    Gross potential of the creatures valued since the first day of the data only, so a creature becoming valued never reads as growth. Their loot values can still change with new evidence.

    Reading the chart

    Chart values and coverage

    All recorded periods at the selected interval. Gold follows Daily avg or Accumulated; activity stays per day. Accumulated sums available daily values inside the selected range; missing days make the subtotal partial. K = thousand, M = million, B = billion on the axes. Scroll for all columns.

    Gross, Gold, NPC and Kills can be shown or hidden independently. Accumulated sums gold inside the selected range, through the latest complete interval, on a logarithmic scale. Gross includes potential NPC conversion; it is not the world's gold balance. Days without data are excluded and partial subtotals are labelled.

    • Daily avg: a day's gold total, or a week's or a month's total over its days. Accumulated: the available gold since the selected range began.
    • Dotted line: the periods in between lack a day of data, or are unfinished. Nothing is drawn for them, and nothing is filled in.
    • Kills per day: every creature kill, valued or not, at the foot of the chart on its own scale.
    • TC Sell Offer, when shown: the median of the world's daily best Sell Offers in each period, on the right scale, in gold per coin. Gold and kills read on the left scale.
    • Dashed: an assumption or a comparison chosen in Assumptions, never an observation.
