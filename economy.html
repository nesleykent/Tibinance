    Assumptions

    Nothing here is observed. Each choice adds a dashed line to the chart and a row to the readout, labelled as what it is.

    Hypothetical NPC liquidation

    Coins plus that share of NPC potential: what would reach the world if players sold that share of their loot to NPCs. No liquidation rate is known, so none is assumed by default.

    Constant creature set

    Gross potential of the creatures valued since the first day of the data only, so a creature becoming valued never reads as growth. Their loot values can still change with new evidence.

    Reading the chart

    Chart values and coverage

    All recorded periods at the selected interval. Gold and activity are daily averages, rounded to whole gold or counts as in the readout; incomplete periods are not plotted. K = thousand, M = million, B = billion on the axes. Scroll for all columns.

    • The measure, in gold per day: a day's total, or a week's or a month's total over its days, so every interval reads on the same scale.
    • Dotted line: the periods in between lack a day of data, or are unfinished. Nothing is drawn for them, and nothing is filled in.
    • Kills per day: every creature kill, valued or not, at the foot of the chart on its own scale.
    • TC Sell Offer, when shown: the median of the world's daily best Sell Offers in each period, on the left scale, in gold per coin.
    • Dashed: an assumption or a comparison chosen in Assumptions, never an observation.
