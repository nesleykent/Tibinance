    Reading the chart

    • Observed best offer: the server day's last observation.
    • Solid line: observations on consecutive server days.
    • Dotted line: the days in between were not observed. Nothing is filled in.
    • Daily average: the average price of the trades that filled this side's offers.
    • Transactions: the raw activity counter for this side during each completed server day. Rolling 30-day Statistics are not daily counts. Actual traded TC quantity is unknown.
    • MEvents, along the foot of the chart: world openings, merges and retirements, updates, XP/Skill and Rapid Respawn events, and market and Store changes. Point at, focus or tap a marker for its days and details. The Events tool opens a calendar, agenda and event details, with a chart marker visibility control.
    • Projection, off until shown: the Research report's offer scenario for this world and side, never an observation. It starts on the last observed best offer, in the tinted days after it. The dashed line is the central path, an equal-weight ensemble of three models for Antica moved to this world's own last best offer, weekly for up to 52 weeks; the faint band is a heuristic stress band, not a confidence interval. It stops where the Research suspends it before an announced merge. Worlds outside the Research's captures, retired worlds and worlds observed after the Research's cutoff have none.

    Sell and Buy, at the top of the chart, choose the side shown; each names its latest best offer, and the figure between them is the spread. The day's high and low in the readout are the highest and lowest prices of that day's trades on the side shown.

    Server days run from 10:00 to 10:00 CET/CEST. Change compares the latest best offer with the last one observed on or before the start of the range.

    The Screener, in the tool rail, sets every world side by side for the side and range chosen: a mini chart of its best offers over the range (solid and dotted as on the chart; the hairline is the value the change is measured from), the latest Sell and Buy Offers and their spread, the change over the range, the mean daily Transactions over the range's days with daily figures (Tx/day), and the latest Captured Depth, the Tibia Coins across every visible offer of the last capture that recorded it (not traded volume). Search, filter by status, PvP type, BattlEye, location and data, and sort by any figure; select a world to open its chart.

    Search reaches retired worlds as well. A retired world keeps its own history, separate from the world it merged into, and its ranges end on its last observed day.
